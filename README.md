# Repackage what we already have

**A demo for civic and community partnerships, University of Bedfordshire, Nov 2026**

**See the page:** https://cefl-foundation.github.io/civic-demo/

A demo web page for University of Bedfordshire colleagues, made for the café on civic and community partnerships. It shows how the Centre for English as a Foreign Language (CEFL) teaches people who start a long way from a subject, who the University could reach in its own towns, abroad and online, the Tech Practice Hub built for that teaching, and how the same tasks could be repackaged for new local audiences.

## The idea

Teaching people a subject in a language they are still learning is what CEFL does every day. For our foundation tech students, that teaching now has a home online: the **CEFL Tech Practice Hub**, a free website of short, guided computing tasks that start where a complete beginner starts.

The Hub can be customised. With new words and new examples, the same tasks could become a first step into computing for people in our own towns who might never have thought of studying it: mature learners, people with additional needs, people still building their English, small businesses and local employers.

From that first step, each person could move on: to a short course, a foundation year, a degree or a master's.

## What the page shows

1. **Who we could reach.** Five made-up people from our own towns, from a bus driver to a care worker, and the first step each of them could take.
2. **What already works.** How the CEFL Tech Practice Hub teaches: very easy tasks first, the words before the code, and access built in for the learner who finds it hardest.
3. **How it can be repackaged.** The same kind of task for three new audiences: people in policing(example), local small businesses, and Computer Science students who need an accessible start (we are already working on that with the Home Foundation team in the School of Computing, Engineering and Creative Industries).
4. **One example in depth.** A path customised for local policing, towards cyber security: the gap between a first step and a degree, a guided trial, five local cases, and why ten minutes fits any rota.
5. **Where it leads.** Many starting points into one network of courses, and who offers what: the first step from CEFL, the degrees and master's courses from the School of Computing, Engineering and Creative Industries.
6. **Already working together.** What CEFL and the School of Computing, Engineering and Creative Industries already share.
7. **Questions and contacts.** Short answers to the questions colleagues ask first, and who to talk to.

## Why it is worth a conversation

- **It already exists.** The CEFL Tech Practice Hub is built, it is being tested with one of our foundation groups this semester, and hosting it is free.
- **Computer Science has already agreed.** The Hub will be shared with their home foundation team, for students with special educational needs, once our testing is finished (end of Nov 2026).
- **It fits real lives.** Ten minutes at a time works around shifts, work and caring.
- **It leads somewhere.** Every path points on to further study.
- **What a pilot would need.** Staff time, a computer room for a few sessions, someone to look after the people who come, and cover for normal teaching while a colleague runs it.

## Good to know

This is a demo for colleagues, not an official University page. The people and cases on it are made up, the three tasks for new audiences are prototypes, and the short courses are ideas for discussion. Policing is one example audience: no police force has been approached.
